## Overview

The site must explain why ACME's Minneapolis facility and production support are useful to clients. Stage specifications, service capabilities, media, logos, and testimonials provide the evidence needed to qualify a project before contact.

## Requirements

### REQ-PROOF-001: Explain stage capability

**User Story:** As a director or producer, I want to understand the LED volume and facility capabilities, so that I can assess whether the stage fits my production.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- **AC-PROOF-001.1:** The Stage experience communicates facility imagery and relevant technical or spatial information.
- **AC-PROOF-001.2:** Stage content distinguishes confirmed capabilities from future or unverified claims.

### REQ-PROOF-002: Explain services

**User Story:** As a producer, I want to understand available production support, so that I can decide whether to book the stage alone or seek end-to-end help.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- **AC-PROOF-002.1:** The Services experience describes the major support categories offered by ACME.
- **AC-PROOF-002.2:** Services content provides a clear path to discuss project needs.

### REQ-PROOF-003: Show credible social proof

**User Story:** As a prospective client, I want to see relevant clients and professional testimonials, so that I can assess trust and market fit.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- **AC-PROOF-003.1:** Approved logo and testimonial records render with attribution.
- **AC-PROOF-003.2:** Proof content remains legible and does not prevent navigation or contact conversion.

## Non-functional requirements

- **NFR-PROOF-001:** Images include meaningful alternative text and externally sourced media failures do not make the page unusable.

## Out of scope

- Claiming additional stage specifications, clients, or services without stakeholder approval.

## Traceability notes

- **Related product goal:** Qualify production clients with accurate evidence.
- **Dependencies:** Public media assets, proof catalog, shared layout, and content approval.
- **Known risks:** Some content may be fixture or draft material.
